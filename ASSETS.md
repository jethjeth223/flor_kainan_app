# Food imagery

The starter food photographs are AI-generated illustrations, not photographs of Flor's actual portions. The interface labels them accordingly. Flor can upload her own food photos from the food catalog.

- Saved asset: public/images/food-atlas.png
- Generation method: built-in image_gen tool.
- Source output: exec-667d1060-6abb-41a0-98ef-29192a0538fe.png, copied into this project.
- The atlas is displayed with CSS background positioning; the generated image has not been edited or cropped into replacement source files.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single seamless food photography atlas used in a Filipino home-cooking app's six starter menu cards. Primary request: a clean 3-column by 2-row photographic contact sheet, every cell exactly equal size, zero gutters, zero borders, no text or watermarks. Each cell shows one centered ceramic bowl or plate on a warm natural cream table, overhead view, consistent soft daylight, appetizing authentic humble Filipino home cooking, editorial food photograph, realistic textures. TOP ROW from left: chicken adobo in glossy soy-vinegar sauce with bay leaf; pork sinigang in clear tamarind broth with kangkong, tomato and radish; pinakbet colorful squash okra eggplant and long beans. BOTTOM ROW from left: crispy lumpiang Shanghai spring rolls and a small dipping sauce; pancit bihon rice noodles with cabbage carrots and calamansi; fluffy steamed white rice in a bowl. All six dishes fully contained in their own cell and same overhead composition. The overall single image aspect ratio is 3:2. Each equal cell square. No food crosses between cells. No lettering, utensils, hands, branding, busy props, collage shadows, or additional dishes. This is a coherent single reusable atlas asset, not a UI mockup.
